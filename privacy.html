Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

us208sync is an OAuth client used only by its owner with the open-source tool rclone.

What data is accessed

With the owner's authorization, us208sync reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive in order to copy, sync and back up those files.

How data is used and stored

Files and access tokens stay on the owner's own computers. No data is sent to any third party, sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
