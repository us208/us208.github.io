us208 Drive Sync

us208 Drive Sync is a private, single-user app that backs up and syncs its owner's files with Google Drive. It uses rclone, an open-source command-line tool for syncing files with cloud storage. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the rclone project.

It is used only by its owner to back up and transfer their own files to and from their own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
